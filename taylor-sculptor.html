A Taylor polynomial is built one term at a time.
Each term you add wraps it tighter around the real curve —
but only near the centre, and sometimes not far at all.

  • 🎯 Match the target curve across the shaded interval, staying inside the green tolerance ribbon.
  • 🔢 Decide how many terms it will take — then commit. The polynomial only appears once you sculpt.
  • 🎖️ You must find the smallest N that fits. Too few won't reach; too many isn't an answer either.
  • 📍 On later levels you choose the centre — where the polynomial is anchored. Choosing well can cut a dozen terms down to three.
  • ⚠️ Some series simply refuse to converge where you want them. Adding terms won't save a badly placed centre.
  • 🏅 Land it first try for a bonus. Misses stay on screen as ghost curves.
